03 / THE ESSENTIALS

Small syntax.
Clear purpose.

01 ::selection

Target the selection.

The double colon introduces a pseudo-element that styles the portion of text selected by the user.

02 color

Set readable text.

Choose the foreground color of selected text. Pair it carefully with the background.

03 background-color

Choose the accent.

Give the selected area its own color. Keep the contrast high enough for easy reading.

04 / YOUR FIRST RULE

One rule changes the feeling.
